About Oakfront Capital

The Trusted Name in Nursery & Childcare M&A

Oakfront Capital is a specialist intermediary in the sale and acquisition of UK nurseries and childcare settings. Our team brings deep sector experience and an unrivalled network of acquirers — from independent operators to national nursery groups.

We understand that the headline value of a sale is not always the most important factor. Cultural fit, continuity of care, staff welfare and deal structure can matter just as much — and we help you navigate every dimension of it.

How We Do It

Selling your nursery is a significant decision — here's how your journey might look

01

Initial Conversation

A confidential, no-obligation call to understand your position and what matters most to you.

02

Information Requested

We take time to understand the numbers, Ofsted rating and occupancy behind your setting.

03

Understand Your Business

Meaningful conversations help us understand your nursery, its people and its culture.

04

Buyer Introductions

We introduce genuinely relevant, vetted acquirers from our off-market network.

05

Heads of Terms

Heads of terms set out the core principles of the deal, giving both sides clarity before further cost is incurred.

06

Due Diligence

A detailed review of finances, compliance, staffing and premises to confirm value and identify issues.

07

Complete Sale

Contracts are signed, funds transferred, and ownership changes hands — smoothly and confidentially.
